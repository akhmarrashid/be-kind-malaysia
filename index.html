Be Kind Malaysia operates through four focused programmes — each targeting a specific group in need, ensuring aid reaches those who need it most.

Distributing monthly food essentials to underprivileged families, ensuring no one goes to bed hungry.

Selangor Food Kit Distribution

Every phase of our Food Kit drive focuses on direct-to-home delivery for urban poor B40 households. In October 2024, our teams successfully packed, distributed, and verified dry pantry supplies directly to low-cost housing schemes across Selangor.

Items provided: 10kg Rice, Cooking Oil, Flour, Condensed Milk, Sugar, Salt, and Canned Protein.

Cancer Financial Aid Support

Medical treatments shouldn't be cut short because of transport fare or basic nutritional constraints. This quarterly framework covers medical out-of-pocket gaps, chemotherapy co-pays, and continuous food support for outpatients dealing with critical income drops.

When monsoons strike, rapid logistical intervention saves lives. Our localized volunteer hubs deployment team brought instant clean drinking water containers, medical hygiene packs, and portable cleaning gear to rural homes affected by severe flooding heights.

Who We Are

A Community Built
on Compassion.

Be Kind Malaysia is a grassroots NGO committed to delivering meaningful, transparent humanitarian aid to those most in need — with a focus on cancer patients and individuals with disabilities.

  • 100% volunteer-driven operations with transparent fund utilisation
  • Verified aid distribution to cancer patients and OKU individuals
  • Rapid response flood relief across Peninsular Malaysia
  • Open to CSR partnerships and corporate sponsorships
